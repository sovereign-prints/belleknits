How it works

  1. You send this form.
  2. We reply with questions, yarn suggestions and a written quote.
  3. You accept, and pay half as a deposit.
  4. We knit, and send you a photo or two along the way.
  5. Balance is settled on completion, then it ships.

Typical timings

Hat or socks — 1 to 2 weeks
Shawl — 3 to 5 weeks
Jersey or cardigan — 6 to 10 weeks
Blanket — 6 to 12 weeks
