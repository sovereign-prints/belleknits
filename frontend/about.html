About

A small studio, a long habit

Overberg Wool Co. began the way most of these things do — with far more yarn than one person could reasonably knit, and a stream of friends asking where to buy it.

The studio stocks natural fibre yarns in small quantities: South African merino, kid mohair and silk, organic cotton, and a hard-wearing sock blend. We buy from growers and dyers we have met, in batches small enough that we can tell you which flock a fleece came off and which pot a skein was dyed in. When a colourway sells out it is genuinely gone until the next dye day.

Alongside the yarn we keep the equipment we actually use ourselves — bamboo and stainless needles, interchangeable sets, brass markers and counters, and project bags built to survive being carried about. Nothing is stocked because it is cheap.

Commissions

A good part of the work is knitting for people who love knitwear but do not knit. Jerseys, shawls, blankets, christening sets and the occasional Fair Isle vest, made to your measurements in yarn we choose together. Every commission starts with a written quote, and nothing is charged until you have accepted it.

Visiting the studio

The studio is open by arrangement rather than on fixed hours — it is a working room, not a shop floor, and we would rather give you an unhurried hour than have you arrive to a locked door. Send a message and we will find a time. Collection of online orders is free and always welcome.

Shipping

  • Courier anywhere in South Africa, 2 to 4 working days.
  • Orders over R1 500 ship free.
  • Yarn is posted in the dye lot you ordered; if we have to split lots we will call first.
  • Commissions ship insured, and we send a tracking number the day it goes.

Returns

  • Unused yarn in its original ball band may be returned within 14 days.
  • Wound or partially used yarn cannot be returned, so please buy a single ball first if you are unsure.
  • Equipment may be returned within 14 days if unused.
  • Bespoke commissions are made to your specification and cannot be returned — which is exactly why we agree everything in writing beforehand.

Yarn care

Nearly everything here wants a cool hand wash and a flat dry. Superwash merino and the sock blend will take a wool cycle. Mohair should be handled gently and never wrung. If in doubt, ask before you wash — it is a short conversation and a long-lived jersey.
